  Missing data and ambiguities:

  1. C131 has no valid yield, because the conversion is wrong. In the source workbook the
     Fluorid (product) row is empty. The 20 mg / 40 % belongs to the Edukt row, which is
     recovered starting material. The converted JSON puts that 40 % on the product. The script
     catches this because 20 mg of the product's molar mass works out to 38.0 %, not 40 %. It
     plots C131 as "n/a" rather than make up a number. C131 needs re-converting or a manual
     fix.
  2. NaBH₄ equivalents in the records are per portion. The standard is 3.2 eq × 2 additions =
     6.4 eq in total. The "12 eq" and "3 eq" in the C119/C120 titles are the rounded totals,
     12.8 and 3.2. C121 and C122 keep the total the same and change only the number of
     portions. I gave them their own group because they don't change the equivalents.
  3. C116's iron catalyst is ambiguous in the source itself. It is named Fe₂(ox)₃·6H₂O, but
     its molar mass (403.99) and mass (105.6 mg) are identical to C114's Fe(NO₃)₃·9H₂O. The
     sheet looks like it was copied from C114. The iron salt and amount actually used are
  4. Confounded runs: in C127–C130 the NH₄OH quench volume changes along with the water/MeCN
     volumes. In C132 the stirring temperature is also RT, as you'd expect. The CSV confounded
     column flags these.
  5. Substrate label: C133 lists "Olefin 145c", while every other run uses "Olefin 091". The
     molar mass is the same, so this is likely a different batch, but it's unconfirmed.
  6. Precision: each condition was run once, with no replicates. Masses are recorded to 1 mg,
     which means about ±1 pp yield resolution. So differences of 2–4 pp are within noise. 
  7. C125: the run differs from the standard only in the _degassed flags on the MeCN additions
     and a note ("all solvents degassed, at least 10 min"). The nitrogen atmosphere is the
     same as the standard.
